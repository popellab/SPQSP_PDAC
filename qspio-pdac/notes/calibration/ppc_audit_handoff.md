## Residual issues (not bugs — design tension)

1. **DFS is structurally 0 in all three scenarios.**
   DFS's definition is `alive AND no RECIST PD from running nadir`. For
   untreated PDAC our model has `recist_pd_at_day_90 = 1` median → by
   day 90 everyone has progressed → DFS at 12/24 mo is 0 by construction.
   **Why deferred:** DFS is only informative with a response window
   (treatment → nadir → later progression). Our neoadjuvant arms deliver
   a single GVAX/nivo dose at day 7 and the effect isn't strong enough
   to move the nadir. Fixable by either (a) modeling surgery + adjuvant
   FOLFIRINOX (issue #19) so there's a post-resection nadir; or (b)
   redefining DFS as "alive AND diameter ≤ baseline × (1 + PD threshold)"
   (alive + no PD from *baseline* rather than nadir).

2. **Scenarios 1 (clinical_progression) and 2 (gvax_neoadjuvant_longterm)
   render near-identical endpoints.**
   GVAX monotherapy at day 7 produces essentially natural-history
   trajectories at 1-3 year horizons. The tiny differences that do
   exist are lost to the ~1 cm of simulator noise. This is consistent
   with real-world evidence — GVAX monotherapy failed in PDAC trials —
   but it makes the audit table look repetitive. **Why deferred:**
   biologically correct, so fixing it requires changing the model's
   mechanistic story, not the reporting layer.

## Follow-on ideas that came up

- **Forest plot visualization** (user-raised). Faceted by endpoint type
  (binary / diameter / TTP) because they don't share a scale. Each
  panel: one row per endpoint, one point per scenario (colored), CI95
  whiskers. Would replace or augment the current markdown table. Entry
  point: add `_plot_predictive_uncertainty` to
  `qsp-inference/src/qsp_inference/audit/plots.py` alongside the
  existing PPC plots; save PNG next to `parameter_audit_report.md` and
  embed via the same relative-path pattern used by
  `_section_target_health`.
- **Extend CI-width headline with absolute width.** The
  `ci95_frac_of_median` rule marks 21 / 36 pairs "undefined" when
  median ≈ 0 (all the binary endpoints land 0 or 1 for most draws). An
  absolute-width column or fallback would give those rows a readable
  uncertainty signal.
- **Stopping-criterion ranking** (originally promised as an "N of 36
  endpoints have CI95 width > X% of their median" line). The current
  headline reports counts but doesn't rank which clinical decisions
  those endpoints feed. Per the handoff in `obed_integration_handoff.md`
  Phase 5, adding this was deferred when we focused on the diameter
  bug. Could emit as a third table: one row per endpoint, sorted by
  CI95 width, with a column naming the clinical decision (approval,
  dose-finding, subtype selection) it feeds.

## What the current audit says

Top drivers of clinical-endpoint uncertainty across all three scenarios
(from `ppc_endpoint_correlations.csv`) are:

| Parameter | Typical |ρ| range | Dominant endpoints |
|---|---|---|
| `k_stroma_support` | 0.17 – 0.32 | OS 24/36 mo, tumor diameter day 730, time-to-progression |
| `f_stroma_max` | 0.13 – 0.32 | tumor diameter day 365/730, RECIST PD, TTP |
| `k_C1_growth` | 0.07 – 0.20 | OS landmarks, TTP |
| `k_C1_death` | 0.11 – 0.17 | RECIST PD, tumor diameter, TTP |
| `k_vas_growth` / `k_vas_decay` | 0.11 – 0.18 | OS 24/36 mo, tumor diameter day 730 |

**If the goal is shrinking clinical predictive uncertainty, these are
the extraction-priority candidates.** The stromal / ECM parameters in
particular are well-suited to `IsolatedSystemTarget` extraction — plenty
of in vitro / ex vivo data exists on PSC activation kinetics and
collagen secretion.
