# GVAX cascade bottleneck — session handoff (2026-04-21)

## TL;DR

The stage-2 NPE posterior (figures/sbi_stage2_current) cannot identify any
of the 12 GVAX-axis parameters — `contraction_local` within ±0.06 of zero
for all of them, even though both GVAX scenarios are in the run. And
scenarios 1 (clinical_progression) and 2 (gvax_neoadjuvant_longterm)
render near-identical clinical predictions at 1–3 years.

The investigation traced the GVAX cascade step-by-step from injection
site → LN priming → circulation → tumor. **LN priming is working
exactly as designed.** The breakdown is at three specific downstream
points:

1. **V_C → V_T trafficking / tumor pool swamping** (dominant).
2. **CD8 arm structurally starved** (mcDC1 unchanged because GVAX
   routes only to mcDC2).
3. **Antigen axis bypass** at the injection site: `V_ID.P1_GVAX ≈ 0.0086 nM`
   vs `EC50_P1_mature = 10 nM`, and `H_P1` in LN doesn't move with GVAX.

Every GVAX-parameter identifiability problem in SBI follows directly from
these — the NPE correctly reports they have no observable effect.

rerunning the qualitative-behaviors helper at median priors for the
`gvax_nivo_neoadjuvant_zheng2022` scenario.

GVAX routes `V_ID.mAPC -> V_LN.mcDC2` only
(`gvax_module.m:275`). mcDC1 is the CD8 cross-presenting subtype;
without an mcDC1-routing mechanism, `H_mAPC` cannot rise and naive CD8
activation stays flat regardless of GVAX dose. CD8 calibration targets
(CD8% FC, CD8-exh%, GzmB-FC) are therefore structurally unreachable.

### Antigen axis bypass

| Quantity | tx_d0 → tx_d21 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| V_ID.P1_GVAX | 0 → 0.0086 nM | 1000× below `EC50_P1_mature = 10 nM` |
| H_P1 (LN antigen Hill) | 0.110 → 0.106 | unchanged |
| H_P0 (self-antigen) | 3.5e-4 → 3.3e-4 | unchanged |

The P1_GVAX concentration at the injection site never approaches
`EC50_P1_mature`, so the inline
`V_ID.P1_GVAX / (V_ID.P1_GVAX + EC50_P1_mature)` factor inside
`J_mature_ID` (`gvax_module.m:169`) is ~10⁻³. Maturation proceeds anyway
because `H_GMCSF_ID ≈ 1` and `k_APC_mature_ID × V_ID.APC` dominates —
but mAPC leaving the injection site carry a negligible antigen load.

Downstream, `H_P1` in LN (generated by `TCR_KPR_module`) doesn't move
with GVAX at all. Even if CD4 trafficking to tumor were fixed, the
TCR-KPR channel wouldn't gate priming on GVAX-derived antigen because
the antigen never reaches the LN mAPC surface in a TCR-detectable form.

## Why SBI can't fix this

All three breakdowns are structural/topological, not parameter choices
within the current priors:

- No amount of tuning `k_GVAX_clearance`, `k_GMCSF_prod`, `EC50_GMCSF`,
  `k_APC_mature_ID`, `k_APC_mig_ID_LN`, `EC50_P1_mature`, `K_APC_mig` can
  move CD8 observables — the CD8 priming Hill is driven by mcDC1, which
  GVAX does not touch.
- Similarly, varying those same parameters cannot produce a >1.04×
  change in V_T.Th or V_T.Treg — the bottleneck is V_C trafficking
  kinetics/tumor pool swamping, downstream of everything the GVAX
  module controls.

The NPE correctly reports these parameters as unidentified because they
have no observable effect on the day-21 calibration targets.

## Proposed fixes

Listed roughly in order of expected impact × effort.

### Fix 1: CD8 cross-priming via antigen transfer to LN-resident cDC1 (HIGH IMPACT)

**Problem.** `H_mAPC` (CD8 priming) uses `V_LN.mcDC1` exclusively
(`APC_module.m:193`). GVAX floods mcDC2 but never touches mcDC1, so
`H_mAPC` cannot respond.

**Biological reality (literature revision 2026-04-21).** The original
framing here ("mcDC2 licenses mcDC1 via CD40L/IL-12/IFNγ") conflated
CD4-help licensing with antigen handoff. The dominant mechanism for
GM-CSF-recruited DC vaccines is actually **antigen transfer from
migratory cDC2/GMDC to LN-resident Batf3⁺ cDC1**, not T-cell licensing.
Two routes exist:

1. **Route A — Direct migratory cDC1 (CD103⁺) pickup at injection site.**
   Tissue cDC1 captures antigen and traffics to LN via CCR7, cross-primes
   CD8 directly. Canonical tumor-antigen route but **weak for GVAX**
   because GM-CSF preferentially expands cDC2/moDCs and is a poor cDC1
   recruiter — this is an acknowledged limitation of GVAX and the
   rationale for newer FLT3L+GM-CSF combination vaccines (Frontiers 2025,
   doi:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1649891).

2. **Route B — Antigen transfer: migratory cDC2/GMDC → LN-resident cDC1.**
   GMDCs migrate poorly but deposit antigen onto resident XCR1⁺ cDC1,
   which then cross-prime. Ferris et al. 2022 *Cancer Immunology Research*
   (PMC9357132) showed **GMDC vaccines fail completely in `Irf8⁺³²⁻/⁻`
   mice** (no host cDC1): "GMDCs do not present antigens directly to host
   T cells, but act as a source of antigen that must be transferred and
   processed by host cDCs." Mueller 2017 *EJI* ("Spreading the load")
   reviews this migratory → resident DC handoff as the general paradigm.

For GVAX specifically, Route B is dominant; Route A is minor. This
explains both (i) the structural failure of CD8 priming in the current
model (no antigen-transfer reaction) and (ii) why GVAX monotherapy
produces only modest CD8 responses clinically.

**Implementation options.**

- *Minimal (recommended):* Add a single reaction
  `V_LN.cDC1 -> V_LN.mcDC1` with rate proportional to the GVAX-derived
  mcDC2 pool (antigen-loaded migrants arriving in LN), abstracting both
  routes into one rate constant. Single new parameter
  `k_cDC1_Ag_transfer`; optionally a Hill on `V_ID.P1_GVAX` to gate on
  antigen availability (couples Fix 1 and Fix 3).
- *More faithful:* Two-channel — (a) small direct migratory cDC1 term
  driven by `H_GMCSF_ID × V_ID.cDC1` (Route A, weak) and (b) dominant
  transfer term driven by `V_LN.mcDC2` (Route B). Adds 2–3 parameters.

**Diagnostic success criterion.** `V_LN.mcDC1` at tx_d21 rises to at
least `n_sites × nCD8_LN × n_CD8_clones / 10` ≈ 13,000 cells to put
H_mAPC above 0.1.

**Key references.**
- Ferris et al. 2022, *Cancer Immunol Res* 10(8):920 (GMDC vaccines
  require host cDC1; primary evidence for Route B dominance).
- Mueller 2017, *Eur J Immunol* (migratory→resident DC antigen transfer
  review).
- Hildner et al. 2008, *Science* 322:1097 (Batf3⁻/⁻ cross-priming
  failure).
- FLT3L+GM-CSF combination vaccine (Frontiers Immunol 2025) — rationale
  that GM-CSF alone is insufficient for cDC1 expansion.

### Fix 2: Logistic carrying-capacity cap on V_T.Th and V_T.Treg (HIGH IMPACT)

**Audit conclusion (2026-04-21).** The trafficking rate constants
(`q_CD4_T_in`, `q_CD8_T_in`) are *not* the bottleneck. All three
V_C→V_T reactions are strictly linear in V_C.{Th,Treg,CD8} — a 54×
V_C.Th bolus delivers 54× the trafficking flux, as designed. The real
bottleneck is the **logistic carrying-capacity cap** on local V_T.Th
and V_T.Treg proliferation:

- `Th_module.m:114–116`:
  `null -> V_T.Th : k_Th_pro_tumor × V_T.Th × (1 - V_T.Th/(rho_Th_max × V_T))`
- `Treg_module.m:190–192`: identical with `rho_Treg_max`.

With `rho_Th_max = 5e6 cell/mL` and `rho_Treg_max = 1e7 cell/mL`, and
baseline V_T.Th/V_T.Treg at or above these caps, the logistic term
goes **negative** as soon as trafficking pushes the pool above the
ceiling, acting as a drain that cancels the GVAX-induced surplus. No
amount of V_C amplification can move the pool substantially past the
cap.

**V_T.CD8 has no local-proliferation term and no cap.** The 1.10×
V_T.CD8 ratio is just passive trafficking of (unchanged) V_C.CD8. Once
Fix 1 unblocks CD8 priming and V_C.CD8 rises, V_T.CD8 will follow
linearly — no Fix-2 change needed for CD8. (LN-side clonal expansion
via `k_CD8_pro × 2^N_aT` already provides the amplification that Th/Treg
get from tumor-local proliferation.)

**Implementation options for Th/Treg.**

- *Minimal (recommended):* Replace the fixed cap with an
  inflammation-scaled capacity, e.g.,
  `rho_Th_max × V_T × (1 + alpha_Th_cap × H_CXCL9_Trec)` and analogous
  for Treg. Adds 1–2 parameters; biologically grounded (inflamed
  tumors sustain higher T cell densities).
- *More aggressive:* Remove logistic cap entirely; rely on
  `k_CD4_death + k_cell_clear + k_Th_to_Treg × H_TGFb` to set pool
  size. Risk: loss of homeostatic stability.
- *Mechanistic:* Gate local proliferation on activation signal (antigen
  + IL-2) rather than density. More work; arguably over-engineering
  when the simple inflammation-scaled cap captures the needed
  responsiveness.

**Diagnostic success criterion.** Under GVAX, V_T.Th rises at least
5–10× (consistent with Ki67+ data in post-GVAX biopsies showing marked
CD4 expansion; Lutz 2014, Le 2015) before plateauing at a new
inflammation-scaled set-point.

### Fix 3: GVAX antigen → LN linkage (MEDIUM IMPACT)

**Problem.** `V_ID.P1_GVAX ≈ 0.009 nM << EC50_P1_mature = 10 nM`, and
GVAX-derived antigen never shows up in the LN P1 pool (`H_P1` doesn't
change under GVAX).

**Options.**

- *Lower EC50_P1_mature* by 2–3 decades (currently 10 nM; move to
  0.01–0.1 nM). Needs a literature check — antigen-dependent APC
  maturation EC50s are typically 0.01–1 nM (estimated range; no primary
  source verified), so
  10 nM is likely too high.
- *Increase y_Ag_per_cell* (`1.7e-8 nmol/cell`) — e.g., if PDAC tumor
  antigen yield per lysed GVAX cell is higher than generic protein yield,
  this parameter can be raised with submodel-target support.
- *Add a mAPC-carried antigen term:* the current LN antigen pool
  (`V_LN.P1`) is populated only from tumor-derived P1 via some
  trafficking/degradation route. Add an explicit term:
  `mAPC migration carries antigen → V_LN.P1` at rate proportional to
  `V_ID.P1_GVAX × k_APC_mig_ID_LN`. This is the biologically correct
  mechanism (DCs physically transport antigen).

### Fix 4: Naive CD4 pool exhaustion (LOW PRIORITY, secondary finding)

`V_LN.nCD4` drops 208 → 112 over 21 days under GVAX. Thymic output /
LN trafficking replenishment isn't keeping up with GVAX-driven
activation consumption. Not a bottleneck yet, but could become one if
Fix 1 + Fix 2 successfully scale the priming rate further.
