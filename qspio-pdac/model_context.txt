# QSPIO-PDAC: Quantitative Systems Pharmacology Model for Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

## Overview
Immuno-oncology QSP model for PDAC capturing tumor-immune-stroma interactions and therapeutic interventions. ~90 species across 11 compartments.

## Compartments
- **V_C**: Central compartment (blood, 5 L)
- **V_P**: Peripheral compartment (tissues, 60 L)
- **V_T**: Tumor compartment (dynamic volume, initially ~1 μL)
- **V_LN**: Tumor-draining lymph node (1113 mm³)
- **V_ID**: Injection site (GVAX administration)
- **V_e**: APC endosomal compartment
- **A_e, A_s**: Endosomal/surface MHC-I compartments
- Synaptic compartments: syn_CD8_C1, syn_CD8_APC, syn_M_C (checkpoint interactions)

## Cell Populations

### Tumor Cells
- **C1**: Viable cancer cells (Gompertzian growth, vasculature-dependent carrying capacity K)
- **C_x**: Dead cancer cells (antigen source, cleared by efferocytosis)

### T Cells (by functional state and compartment)
- **nCD8, nCD4**: Naive T cells (V_C, V_P, V_LN)
- **aCD8, aTreg, aTh**: Activated T cells (V_LN, primed by mature DCs)
- **CD8, Treg, Th**: Effector T cells (V_T, V_C, V_P, V_LN)
- **CD8_exh, Th_exh**: Exhausted T cells (V_T) — PD-1hi, reduced function

### Dendritic Cells (two subtypes)
- **cDC1**: Immature conventional DC type 1 (BATF3+, cross-presenting → CD8 priming via MHC-I)
- **cDC2**: Immature conventional DC type 2 (IRF4+, MHC-II → CD4 Th/Treg priming)
- **mcDC1, mcDC2**: Mature (activated) forms of each subtype
- Present in both V_T and V_LN (8 species total)
- Maturation driven by DAMP or IL-12; inhibited by IL-10 and TGFb; blocked by H_pore_APC (ECM exclusion)
- Aggregate aliases: APC_total_T, mAPC_total_T, mAPC_total_LN (backward compatibility)

### NK Cells
- **NK**: NK cells in V_T; **NK_P**: NK cells in V_P
- Secrete IFNg and XCL1 (cDC1 recruitment); killing suppressed by IL-10, TGFb, MDSC

### Myeloid Cells
- **Mac_M1**: M1 macrophages (pro-inflammatory, anti-tumor, secrete IL-12)
- **Mac_M2**: M2 macrophages (immunosuppressive, secrete IL-10)
- **MDSC**: Myeloid-derived suppressor cells (secrete NO, ArgI; recruited by CCL2, IL-6)

### Stromal Cells — CAF Subtypes (PDAC-specific)
- **qPSC**: Quiescent pancreatic stellate cells (dormant precursor)
- **iCAF**: Inflammatory CAFs — secrete IL-6, CCL5; IL-1-driven; TGFb antagonizes via IL-1R1 downregulation
- **myCAF**: Myofibroblastic CAFs — secrete collagen, CXCL12, TGFb, VEGF; TGFb-driven
- **apCAF**: Antigen-presenting CAFs — tolerogenic; sequester antigen P1, induce Tregs

### ECM / Desmoplasia
- **collagen**: Fibrillar collagen mass (mg) in V_T; produced by myCAFs, degraded by MMPs (M1-dependent) and constitutive turnover
- **phi_collagen**: Collagen volume fraction (dimensionless, 0–1) — primary ECM state variable
- **d_pore**: Dynamic pore diameter (μm) — `d_pore_ref * (phi_col_ref / phi_collagen)^0.5`
- **stiffness**: Tissue stiffness (kPa-scaled) — `E_ref * (phi_collagen / phi_col_ref)^n_stiff`
- **H_pore_T, H_pore_APC**: Pore-size-based immune cell exclusion
- **H_stiff_Texh**: Stiffness-mediated T cell exhaustion
- **H_stiff_fib**: Stiffness-mediated fibroblast activation (YAP/TAZ mechanotransduction)

## Checkpoint Molecules (synaptic compartments)
- **PD-1/PD-L1/PD-L2**: T cell exhaustion axis
- **CTLA-4/CD80/CD86**: T cell activation control
- **CD28/CD80/CD86**: Co-stimulation
- **CD47/SIRPα**: "Don't eat me" signal (macrophage phagocytosis checkpoint)
- Multi-valent binding kinetics with ~31 species per synapse

## Therapeutic Interventions
- **Checkpoint inhibitors**: aPD1 (nivolumab), aPDL1 (atezolizumab), aCTLA4 (ipilimumab/tremelimumab)
- **Vaccine**: GVAX (GM-CSF-secreting tumor cells → cDC2 maturation at injection site)
- **Chemotherapy**: nab-paclitaxel (3-compartment PK)
- **Epigenetic**: entinostat (HDAC inhibitor)
- **Targeted**: cabozantinib (anti-angiogenic), anti-CD47 (phagocytosis checkpoint)
- **Immunomodulatory**: low-dose cyclophosphamide (Treg depletion)

## Key Parameter Naming Conventions
- `k_*`: Rate constants (e.g., k_C1_growth, k_Treg_death)
- `K_*`: Carrying capacities or association constants
- `*_50`: EC50 / half-maximal concentrations (e.g., TGFb_50_APC, IL10_50)
- `n_*`: Hill coefficients (e.g., n_stiff, n_pore)
- `q_*`: Transport/trafficking rates (e.g., q_CD8_T_in)
- `rho_*`: Density/carrying capacity parameters (e.g., rho_Treg_max)
- `f_*`: Fraction parameters (e.g., f_stroma_max, f_col_basal)
- `d_*`: Diameter/distance parameters (e.g., d_pore_ref, d_crit_T)
- `E_*`: Stiffness parameters (e.g., E_ref, E_50_Texh)
- `phi_*`: Volume fraction parameters (e.g., phi_col_ref)
- `APC0_*`: Homeostatic DC densities (e.g., APC0_cDC1_T, APC0_cDC2_LN)

## PDAC-Specific Biology
1. **Desmoplastic stroma**: Dense collagen matrix produced by myCAFs creates physical barriers (pore exclusion) and mechanical signaling (stiffness-driven exhaustion)
2. **CAF subtype plasticity**: IL-1/TGFb axis drives iCAF↔myCAF interconversion; apCAFs are tolerogenic
3. **T cell exclusion**: CXCL12 from iCAFs/cancer cells prevents T cell infiltration
4. **Layered immunosuppression**: MDSC (NO, ArgI) + M2 macrophages (IL-10) + Tregs (CCL5-recruited) + TGFb
5. **Positive feedback loops**: IL-6 autocrine on iCAFs; IL-12→NK→XCL1→cDC1→IL-12
